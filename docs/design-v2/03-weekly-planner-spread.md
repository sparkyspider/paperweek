# 03 — Weekly planner spread reference

**Status: SUPERSEDED by ref 04 (`04-perfect-weekly-planner.md`) — Mark named 04 "the perfect look and feel" for V2. Kept for comparison only. Where 03 and 04 differ, 04 wins.**

Reference image: `03-weekly-planner-spread.png` (supplied by Mark, 2026-09-01). Same quiet-sketchbook family as ref 02 — this is the PLANNER view companion to 02's login. Mark: "Note this."

## What it shows

A spiral-bound weekly planner spread, cream paper: seven day columns (Mon–Sun), each divided by fine rules into **Morning / Afternoon / Evening / Notes** — exactly Paperweek's four sections. Tasks are handwritten bullets sitting on faint ruled lines.

## Design cues worth extracting

- **Header row**: month + year top-left ("May 2024", ink underline flourish); centre — the week range "May 20 — May 26" written on a strip of washi tape, flanked by hand-drawn ← → arrows (week navigation!); top-right a small calendar glyph and "Today" with a yellow highlighter swipe.
- **Day headers**: small-caps day name over a large date numeral with a **yellow highlighter swipe underneath** — charming, minimal.
- **Section labels**: "Morning / Afternoon / Evening / Notes" written small in handwriting at the head of each section with a fine rule under — NOT watermarks (contrast with v1's current watermark approach; discuss which wins).
- **Task idiom**: bullet-dot + handwriting on a line. Some tasks carry a small ballot-box (to-do) instead of a bullet. Emphasis via underline swipes in **red/pink** (urgent?), **green** (health/plan), plus tiny ink doodles (asterisk, sun, heart) as personality.
- **Colour-dot legend** (bottom-left): Work = blue dot, Personal = pink, Health = green, To-do = ballot box. → Directly maps to Paperweek's life-themes: theme = a small ink dot beside the task instead of any chip.
- **Notes section**: holds taped/paper-clipped **sticky notes** (kraft, cream, pink) with handwritten quotes — notes as physical scraps rather than more lines. Also small ink doodles (flower vase) as decoration.
- **Weekend treatment**: Sat/Sun columns slightly warmer/tinted — matches v1's weekend-wash decision.
- **Margins**: motivational handwriting in the margins ("plan with intention / design your days", "Make time for what matters."), ink splatters, a fountain pen resting on the page.

## Relation to v1 (already-built day-strip)

Strong overlaps — v1 already has: four sections, ruled lines, handwriting (Caveat), weekend wash, Today affordance, no chips. Divergences to discuss:
- Section labels: small handwritten headings-with-rule (this ref) vs big faint right-aligned watermarks (v1 current).
- Week-at-a-glance 7-column page vs v1's endless horizontal day strip; the washi-tape week label + arrows could adapt to the strip as its header/navigation.
- Theme colour dots per task (this ref's legend) — candidate for v1.5 already, it's cheap and chip-free.
- Highlighter swipes (yellow date underlines, red/green task emphasis) vs v1's warm sienna accents.
- Sticky-note Notes section vs plain ruled Notes.

## Open questions for the discussion

- Bring back a week-page (7 columns fixed) as an alternate zoom level of the strip?
- Theme dots: colour-only legend (like this ref) or dot + tooltip?
- Sticky notes in Notes: charming or clutter? (Mark's rule: fun but by no means messy.)
