## The full recipe (consolidated — supersedes 03's cue list where different)

- Cream paper, fine sepia ruling, small handwritten section labels ("Morning / Afternoon / Evening / Notes") each with a fine rule beneath — labels in the flow, NOT watermarks.
- Day header: small-caps day name, big date numeral, **yellow highlighter swipe** under the numeral.
- Month + year top-left with ink scribble underline; motivational margin handwriting; "Make time for what matters." bottom-right.
- Tasks: bullet-dot (or small ballot box for to-dos) + handwriting on the line; emphasis = highlighter/underline swipes (red/pink, green); tiny ink doodles as personality.
- Colour-dot theme legend bottom-left (maps to Paperweek life-themes as tiny ink dots).
- Notes section: taped/clipped sticky notes (kraft/cream/pink) with handwritten quotes.
- Fixed 7-column week spread (Mon–Sun) on one page — a "week page" zoom level (vs v1's endless strip; discuss whether V2 keeps the strip, the page, or both as zoom levels).
- Physicality: spiral binding, page sits in a photographed-desk world, fountain pen resting on the page, controlled ink splatters.
